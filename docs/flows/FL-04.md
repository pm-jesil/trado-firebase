# FL-04: Direct Vendor Purchase & Payment Voucher

> **Domain:** Finance, Stock & Account Integrity (Prompt 77)  
> **Personas:** `purchaser`, `finance_manager`  
> **Key Invariants:** `INV-1`, `INV-2`, `INV-3`, `INV-4`  
> **Callables:** `outflowsCreate`, `accountEntriesList`  

---

## 1. Overview
Purchasing stock with immediate cash or bank payment. Stock increments and operating account is debited.

## 2. Executable Steps & Request Payloads

### Step 1: Create purchase outflow with immediate payment
- **Callable:** `outflowsCreate`
- **Request Payload:**
```json
{
  "kind": "purchase",
  "vendorId": "vend_1",
  "totalAmount": 150000,
  "paidAmount": 150000,
  "paymentMethod": "bank_transfer"
}
```

### Step 2: Verify vendor purchase total and bank balance reduction
- **Callable:** `accountEntriesList`
- **Request Payload:**
```json
{
  "sourceDocumentType": "outflow"
}
```

- **INV-3:** Must hold before and after each transaction in this flow.
- **INV-4:** Must hold before and after each transaction in this flow.
